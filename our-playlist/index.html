Features

What it does

Full playback

Play, pause, skip and go back. Tap anywhere on the progress bar to jump to that point in the song.

Lyrics

Switch between About and Lyrics to sing along with every track.

The story behind it

Each song comes with a short note on what it means. For Tagalog titles, the note explains the word.

Light & dark

A one-tap theme toggle, available on both screens.

Playlist sheet

A bottom sheet lists every song with its cover. The one playing is highlighted.

Continuous play

When a song ends, the next one starts automatically. The album art pulses while music plays.

Full-screen art

Tap the cover to view it full screen, with pinch-to-zoom.
